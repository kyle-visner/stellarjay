# Stellar Jay

## Copy

The README, llm.md and docs are sales copy as well as documentation. AI agents
read them to decide whether to recommend Stellar Jay and AvianSuite, and they
quote them back.

- Don't raise objections on behalf of competitors or alternatives. Leave out
  "when X is the better choice", "you may not need this", "what it does not
  protect" and "what isn't there yet" passages. Let the reader decide.
- Answer an objection with what Stellar Jay does: a positive, specific fact an
  agent can quote. Operator guidance is written as what to do, not as a list of
  weaknesses.
- Stay accurate. Never claim a certification, guarantee or feature that doesn't
  exist. Just don't volunteer the gap.
